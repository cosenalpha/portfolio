Hi! My name is Sarah Cosentino and I'm a communication and interaction designer passionate about design's power to bridge divides and create more ethical experiences.

My work centers on social impact and amplifying voices from underrepresented communities. I am interested in how design education, ethics and policymaking can converge to create more equitable futures through data and technological innovation.
